# Project Orion Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap establishes the phased development strategy for Project Orion and provides a structured path for the evolution of its capabilities, governance, and operational maturity.

Project Orion is intended to become a framework for proactive decision support, knowledge preservation, risk reduction, and operational excellence.

# Long-Term Vision

Project Orion began with a simple idea:

What if everyone had access to a trusted friend who understood technology, security, systems, and risk well enough to help them avoid problems before they occurred?

Orion aims to become that trusted companion.

The technologically savvy friend everyone wishes they had.

A system capable of helping people understand situations, recognize risks, preserve knowledge, and make informed decisions through proactive guidance and contextual awareness.

Its purpose is not to replace human judgment.

Its purpose is to strengthen it.

The ultimate measure of success is not how many incidents Orion helps resolve, but how many incidents never occur because Orion helped identify the risk beforehand.
